# Payanam Vision & Philosophy

Last Updated: 2026-07-24

## Core Vision

**Payanam** (பயணம் — "journey") is a privacy-first life dimension manager. It helps you:

- **Track** — tasks, habits, time, and notes across the dimensions of life you define
- **See patterns** — how your energy, focus, and priorities shift over time
- **Reflect** — a private mirror for your own growth, not a productivity dashboard for someone else's goals

All data stays on your device. No cloud, no accounts, no tracking — unless you choose otherwise.

---

## Principles

1. **Privacy by default** — Architecture enforces it, not just promises.
2. **AGPL-3.0** — All improvements stay open. No closed forks.
3. **Local-first** — Your data lives on your device. Sync is optional, e2ee, and self-hostable.
4. **User-controlled dimensions** — You define what matters. Not me, not an algorithm.

---

## What Payanam Is Not

- ❌ **Not a data collection platform** — No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking.
- ❌ **Not a SaaS** — No accounts required. No vendor lock-in.
- ❌ **Not a Maximizer** — Does not impose goals. Your life, your dimensions.

---

## Boundaries

| Welcome | Not interested |
|---------|----------------|
| Bug fixes & features that strengthen privacy | Breaking architecture changes |
| Documentation & localization | Heavy dependencies that undermine local-first |
| Performance & testing improvements | Telemetry without explicit opt-in |
| Independent forks (AGPL-compliant) | Cloud sync without e2ee |

---

## Who This Is For

- Developers learning Kotlin, Compose, and modular Android architecture
- Privacy-conscious users who want a local-first life tracker
- Anyone curious about the intersection of personal analytics and open source
- Forkers and builders who want a solid foundation to extend

---

## The Business Side (Transparent)

The app is **AGPL-3.0** and free. Future optional services (e2ee cross-device sync, cloud AI inference for devices that can't run models locally) are where I hope to sustain the project — keeping the core open, local, and free while offering convenience layers for those who want them.

If that doesn't fit your needs, the AGPL license gives you everything you need to fork, modify, and distribute your own version.
